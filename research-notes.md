## "Typical slate window" estimates (all-30-clubs days)
With all 30 MLB clubs counting, a regular-season day has games across North America. Typical union of
first pitch → last out in PT: Mon/Tue/Fri 3:35 PM–10:00 PM; Wed/Thu 9:35 AM–10:00 PM (getaway day games);
Sat 10:05 AM–10:00 PM; Sun 10:05 AM–7:00 PM (incl. Sunday Night Baseball 4 PM PT). Spring training days:
~10:00 AM–4:30 PM (FL 1:05 ET starts = 10:05 PT; AZ 1:05 MST starts). These are ESTIMATES used only for
day-level free-time display; season boundary dates themselves are verified (2027) or pattern estimates (2028+).
NFL Sundays in season: 10:00 AM–8:45 PM PT (1 PM ET window through SNF). NCAAF Saturdays (Stanford/Cal):
kickoff windows typically 12:00–7:30 PM PT starts → busy 12:00 PM–7:30 PM+3:26 on game Saturdays (estimated
for 2027+; exact for the verified 2026 remainder).

## The vacation math (strict "Core 4" definition) — engine-verified 2026-11-02
A day is fully free only if NO MLB (incl. spring training), NO NFL (incl. Westwood One + Pro Bowl Games),
and NO Stanford/Cal game is on. Binding constraints each year:
- NFL regular season runs every Thu/Sun/Mon (+Sat late) early Sep–early Jan → best in-season gap = Tue–Wed (2 days).
- NFL playoffs run weekends only (Sat/Sun/+Mon Wild Card) → weekday "corridors" appear mid-Jan–mid-Feb,
  chopped by the Conference-Championship, Pro Bowl and Super Bowl Sundays into ≤6-day stretches.
- MLB spring training games begin 5 days after the Super Bowl; regular season late Mar–end Sep; postseason Oct.
- NCAAF takes most Saturdays late Aug–late Nov.
Computed top strictly-free streaks (engine = docs/data.js; Node test run, all assertions passed):
- 2027: Jan 25–30, Feb 1–6, Feb 8–13 (6 days each; boundaries est. except SB) + Feb 15–18 (4 days;
  BOTH boundaries verified: SB LXI Feb 14 ← NFL; spring games Feb 19 ← official 2027 MLB schedule).
- 2028: est. Jan 24–29, Jan 31–Feb 5, Feb 7–12 (6d) + Feb 14–17 (4d). CONDITIONAL: Jul 12–20, 2028 (9d)
  if MLB's envisioned extended Olympic break is adopted (scenario toggle; not agreed as of Jul-2026 reporting).
- 2029: est. Jan 22–27, Jan 29–Feb 3, Feb 5–10 (6d) + Feb 12–15 (4d).
No 7/14/21-day strict window exists in 2027–2029 (max 6 days, conditional 2028-July aside).
