# Independent vacation-safety audit — 2026-11-03

Sources below were consulted through web search or the official page; a link alone does not establish a game-specific broadcast, a clear minute, or coverage in 94122.

## Findings requiring integration

1. **Busy minutes double-count simultaneous events.** `compute_vacation_windows` in `scripts/collect_official.py` adds `end - start` for every interval rather than the length of their union. On the current published snapshot, the 2027-02-03–2027-02-16 candidate displays 3,944 busy minutes; merging overlapping day intervals yields 2,504 wall-clock minutes. For 2027-01-27–2027-02-16 it displays 7,364 rather than 4,304. The published ranking uses these sums as a tie-breaker. Recomputing the nine currently selected candidate starts with the union did not change their dates, but the displayed numbers are wrong. This is not an estimate uncertainty; it is an arithmetic error. Suggested fix: union per-day blocks before calculating busy minutes and use that same value in ranking; add overlapping/nested/adjacent regression tests and regenerate. Official source for the 2027 Super Bowl anchor, not the arithmetic: <https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-27-national-football-league-important-dates>.

2. **The game-specific Giants station field is empty for most Giants rows; A's terrestrial and streaming are conflated.** `build_mlb_events` checks `"Giants" in (away, home)` even though the team name is `San Francisco Giants`. In the committed `events.json`, 377 of 393 Giants MLB rows have no station; the 16 against the Athletics show only `960 AM KNEW / A's Cast`. All 387 Athletics rows have that same combined label, regardless of game or year. The [Giants' official network page](https://www.mlb.com/giants/schedule/broadcast-partners) identifies KNBR 680 AM/104.5 FM as flagship, but does **not** establish carriage for each game; the [Giants' 2025 spring training FAQ](https://www.mlb.com/giants/news/giants-spring-training-2025-faq) demonstrates that not every spring broadcast is necessarily on KNBR. The [Athletics' affiliate page](https://www.mlb.com/athletics/schedule/affiliates) identifies KNEW as a Bay Area affiliate, while their [A's Cast FAQ](https://www.mlb.com/athletics/fans/podcasts/faq) describes A's Cast as online streaming. Neither establishes reception or that every future/spring game is carried by KNEW. Fix the team-name test, separate terrestrial from streaming and verify individual carriage; leave game-specific station blank/unverified absent evidence. Do not simply assign a flagship to every game.

3. **Model/unknown 'day' counters count blocks, not days.** The inner interval loop increments `model_days` and `unverified_days` per interval, even when multiple blocks occur on the same date. In the 2027-02-03–2027-02-16 result `unverified_days` is 2 but only one date has an unverified block; in the 21-day result it is 4 versus 2 distinct dates. The same problem can affect `model_days`, which also affects ranking. Count distinct dates and test two same-status blocks on a day. These are local code/data observations, not official-source claims; consult the [WWO station finder](https://www.westwoodonesports.com/station-finder/) for its explicit warning that affiliates do not carry every event.

4. **Incomplete snapshot and future schedules prevent certification.** A no-block or low-conflict period cannot establish an absence of radio programming; annual/season-level links cannot verify derived gap values. The official NFL page confirms Super Bowl LXI on February 14, 2027, but not that February 15–18 is free; official WWO warns about local blackout and scheduling conflicts. No 7-, 14-, or 21-day radio-free vacation is certified here. See <https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-27-national-football-league-important-dates> and <https://www.westwoodonesports.com/station-finder/>. Official [Orioles 2027 schedule](https://www.mlb.com/press-release/press-release-orioles-announce-2027-regular-season-schedule) confirms a March 25 opening and dates, not automatically every listed local radio broadcast. The data snapshot is dated November 2, 2026 and needs an accountable refresh pipeline.

5. **DST elapsed-time limitation.** Daily calculations represent every date as 1,440 wall-clock minutes, even on Pacific daylight-saving transition days, and game durations are added to wall-clock minutes. Wall-clock display and elapsed duration differ across the skipped or repeated hour. The dashboard should either calculate with timezone-aware instants and represent repeated/skipped hours or explicitly flag affected dates as unknown. Reference: <https://www.nist.gov/pml/time-and-frequency-division/popular-links/daylight-saving-time-dst>.
