## Correction and residual work

The primary page templates and metadata withdraw the misleading recommendation and prominently disclose the missing Cal game with an official-source table on Flagged for Review. The game is **not yet integrated** into the daily interval calculations, and the other Cal and Warriors games are not exhaustively ingested by this change. There is no live data refresh in the root dashboard. Existing source links, model output, historical audits, and archived editions do not certify complete official-source verification or minute-exact real-world availability. A further integration must ingest and independently check each applicable game, deduplicate, recompute day/holiday/vacation windows with uncertainty, check radio carriage and reception separately, reverify every row, and reconcile open PR #18 without discarding its work. No 7-, 14-, or 21-day trip is currently certified free.
