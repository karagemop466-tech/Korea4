Read with care

  • Rest effects are observational: travel, opponent strength, injuries and game quality are not adjusted in the simple subgroup means.
  • The home win-rate 95% range uses a large-sample proportion approximation; ties are coded as one-half.
  • Multiple comparisons exist; no Holm correction has been applied to these exploratory summaries.
  • Game margins are noisy. The model residual standard deviation is an empirical snapshot, not the assumed sigma parameter in the fair-line engine.
