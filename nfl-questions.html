Questions

Known, unknown, contradictory and required information before a reasonable decision can be made.

Decision framework: Known values are distinguished from missing information. OPEN questions have no default answer. Contradictory claims are explicitly called out in the relevant question; verify at source before resolving. The research path is a suggestion, not evidence that the missing value has been obtained.

D-01 · Which bet classes the program is allowed to originate

OPEN

Question: Is the program scoped to singles, parlays and player props, or to a subset?

Why it matters: Each class needs different official inputs, different gates and different settlement rules. The player-prop class currently has no per-player usage data.

Known
  • Ten strategy definitions covering all three classes (data/nfl/strategies.json).
  • Official schedule for all 272 games of the 2026 regular season, parsed from the official by-week snapshots.
  • Official Week 10 injury report snapshot with 298 rows across all 32 clubs, including two official no-injury rows.
Unknown / contradictory
  • Official per-player usage data needed for priced player props.
  • A decision on whether exclusion-only prop coverage is acceptable.

Research path: Capture official NFL player statistics and game books, or accept the exclusion-only scope (suggestion SG-12).

D-02 · Where prices come from

OPEN

Question: Which source supplies the line and the price at the moment a line is originated, and who records it?

Why it matters: Without a price there is no edge, no ROI and no leaderboard points. Gates G2, G5, G9 and G12 are all blocked on this one question.

Known
  • Official sources publish schedules, results, injury reports, standings and rules.
  • No official source publishes an NFL betting line or price.
Unknown / contradictory
  • A price feed or the owner's own bet log with price and timestamp for each bet.

Research path: Owner bet log (recommendation SG-10 option A) or a licensed feed (option B).

D-07 · Model parameters that are assumptions rather than measurements

OPEN

Question: Are the assumed margin standard deviation, the Pythagorean exponent, the home-edge points and the availability penalty table accepted as program parameters, and who may change them?

Why it matters: These parameters move every published line, so they are part of the model and must be versioned and disclosed.

Known
  • Measured official values: the league home win rate and home margin from 120 official completed games, and the availability penalty table as published.
  • The measured year-to-year persistence of points differential (r = 0.32 on 32 complete official team-season pairs, 2024 to 2025).
Unknown / contradictory
  • Owner acceptance of the assumed margin standard deviation of 12 points, which is used to convert a win probability into a point margin.

Research path: Owner decision; the published sensitivity of each line to this parameter bounds the impact.

D-16 · Multi-strategy correlation policy

OPEN

Question: What correlation limit applies between legs of one ticket, and what counts as a separate game?

Why it matters: Correlated legs multiply variance rather than edge; the measured correlation matrix is currently based on one week and is unstable.

Known
  • Measured signal correlations on the captured week and the rule that legs must have absolute correlation at or below 0.30 with at most four legs.
