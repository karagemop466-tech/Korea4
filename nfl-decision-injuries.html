What is still missing for offense and defense backup tracking

  • Depth charts for the other 31 clubs (only the 49ers chart is captured) — decision D-05.
  • Game-day inactives, published by clubs on game day, which is the only official confirmation of who actually sat.
  • Injured-reserve moves, published in club news feeds; without them, depth is understated.
  • Snap counts, which the league's public stat pages do not publish — so "who actually played" is not answerable from official stats alone.
