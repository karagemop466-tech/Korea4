S-01 · Starter-loss availability edge (spread)

GATED · 3/12 checks passed

G1 · Official-source integrity — PASS

Every input value comes from an official league, team or network source and every row carries that source URL. No secondary aggregator, no news summary, no inferred value.

Injury designations read from the official NFL weekly injury report; depth tiers read from official team depth charts. Depth charts captured this session: San Francisco 49ers, Miami Dolphins, Baltimore Ravens.

G2 · Minimum sample size — NOT TESTABLE

At least 100 settled, priced bets for the strategy before it may be scored or ranked.

13 originated games in the captured week; no settled priced bets recorded.

G3 · Out-of-sample (walk-forward) test — NOT TESTABLE

Performance is measured on games that were played after the signal timestamp; no random shuffle, no in-sample fitting, no feature computed from information published after the line was originated.

Only the current week has been captured; a walk-forward panel needs earlier injury weeks plus results.

G4 · Multiple-testing correction — NOT TESTABLE

The number of strategies tested is declared and a Holm-Bonferroni correction (or deflated Sharpe ratio) is applied to the significance threshold before a strategy is called profitable.

Catalog size is declared (10 strategies); the Holm-Bonferroni step runs once samples exist.

G5 · Statistical significance and confidence interval — NOT TESTABLE

The 95% bootstrap confidence interval for expected edge must exclude zero in the positive direction, and the number of bets must exceed G2.

No settled bets to bootstrap.

G6 · Luck / variance control — NOT TESTABLE

The signal is tested against a variance-matched null: the edge must survive controlling for opponent strength, schedule features and one-game noise, and the persistence measured from official season data must explain less variance than the signal itself.

Season-to-year persistence is measured from official standings (see the correlation page) and must be used as the noise baseline before this strategy can be judged.

G7 · Correlation and effective sample size — PARTIAL

Legs combined in one ticket must have measured |rho| <= 0.30, the ticket is capped at 4 legs, and the reported sample size is the effective sample size n_eff = n / (1 + (k-1) * mean_rho).

10 measured signal correlations from the captured week.

G8 · Robustness across sub-periods and thresholds — NOT TESTABLE

Positive result in at least 60% of seasons tested, and the sign of the edge does not flip when the entry threshold is moved within the stated band.

Needs multiple seasons.

G9 · Price availability and line-movement discipline — NOT TESTABLE

A bet is only recorded when a price exists at the origination timestamp, with the bookmaker/price source and timestamp stored; the line must have been stable for the stated minimum window.

No price source is configured; see decisions register D-03.

G10 · Risk limits — PASS

Kelly fraction per bet <= 0.25, weekly exposure cap, and no parlay longer than 4 legs or combining two legs from the same game.

Kelly fraction <= 0.25 and no same-game combination are configured in the leaderboard rules.

G11 · Provenance and disclosure — PASS

Every bet record stores the input values, their official source URLs and the capture timestamps, and the site publishes the model parameters used.

Every deficiency row stores the official injury URL and the official depth-chart URL.

G12 · Settlement integrity — NOT TESTABLE

Bets settle on the official final score from an official source, with pushes handled as pushes and voided games excluded.

No originated line has settled yet.
