📋 Project Overview

This planner tracks all live sports broadcasts receivable on standard AM/FM radio in San Francisco's Outer Sunset neighborhood (ZIP 94122). The goal is to identify the best vacation windows — periods of 1, 2, or 3+ consecutive weeks where no MLB, NFL, or NCAAF games are being broadcast live.

Sports Tracked

  • MLB — All 30 teams, 162 regular-season games each + postseason. SF Giants and Athletics flagged high priority.
  • NFL — All games, preseason through Super Bowl. SF 49ers flagged high priority.
  • NCAAF — Stanford Cardinal and Cal Golden Bears flagged high priority.
  • Westwood One Sports — National NFL radio broadcasts (MNF, TNF, SNF, Thanksgiving, Christmas, Playoffs).

🏖️ Best Vacation Windows by Year

2026

  • Late October - Early November (~10 days): After World Series ends (Oct 28) and before NFL Week 9 intensifies. NCAAF season ongoing (Saturdays busy). ✓ Verified dates
  • Mid-November bye periods: Some mid-week gaps exist between NCAAF ending (late Nov) and NFL playoffs starting (mid Jan). Weekdays (Tue-Fri) often free during NFL season outside of TNF.

2026-2027 Offseason (Best Overall)

⚠️ Important Constraints

  • During MLB season (late March - late October): Games are played nearly every day. It is essentially impossible to find a full week free of MLB broadcasts. Some individual weekdays may be free if no local games are scheduled.
  • During NFL season (September - January): Sundays, Mondays, and Thursdays consistently have games. Tuesday and Wednesday are typically the only consistently free weekdays.
  • During NCAAF season (late August - early December): Saturdays are consistently busy. Friday nights occasionally have games.
  • The "perfect storm" period: September - October has MLB, NFL, and NCAAF all running simultaneously. This is the busiest time of year.
  • The best overall strategy: Plan vacations during February-March (post-Super Bowl, pre-MLB) for maximum free time.
