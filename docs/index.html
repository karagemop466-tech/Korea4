📌 Executive summary

The short answer: under your strict rules — zero MLB (all 30 clubs, incl. spring training & postseason), zero NFL (incl. 49ers + Westwood One), zero Stanford/Cal football on the radio — there is no 1-, 2- or 3-week vacation window in 2027, 2028 or 2029. The longest the dial ever goes fully dark is 6 consecutive days — and that only happens in the late-January/mid-February “playoff corridor,” in the weekday seams between NFL playoff weekends and the Super Bowl. Everywhere else, baseball hands off to football and football hands off to spring training.

🎯 What “free” means here (your Core 4)

A minute is free when none of these are on the radio:

  • MLB — all 30 clubs, regular + postseason + All-Star + spring training (Giants ★ & A's ★ flagged high priority)
  • NFL — all games incl. preseason/postseason; 49ers ★ high priority
  • Stanford ★ and California ★ NCAA football
  • Westwood One national NFL radio (Thursday Night Football/SNF/MNF, playoffs, Super Bowl)

Everything else actually receivable on an AM/FM radio in 94122 — Warriors, Sharks, Valkyries, Earthquakes, the College Football Playoff on ESPN Radio, Westwood One's NCAA hoops/March Madness, LA28 Olympics — is tracked as an optional second tier. Flip on “All Bay Area radio sports” on the Scoreboard to count those as busy too.

🔎 How to read the tables

VERIFIED = checked line-by-line against an official source (linked in the row).   ESTIMATE = no official schedule exists yet; the value is modeled from a verified pattern and says so.   UNVERIFIED = could not be confirmed from any official source — left blank or flagged, never invented.   ★ HIGH PRIORITY = Giants, A's, 49ers, Stanford, Cal.
