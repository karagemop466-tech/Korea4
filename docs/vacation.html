FREE TIME VACATION 📻

The question: find 1, 2 or 3 straight weeks with no MLB, no NFL/Westwood One, no Stanford/Cal on the radio. Here is the honest answer.

The headline finding: a strict 7-, 14- or 21-day zero-conflict window does not exist in 2027, 2028 or 2029 under the Core-4 rules (which count spring training, all 30 MLB clubs and the NFL's all-star exhibition). The maximum anywhere is 6 consecutive days — except one conditional 9-day case in July 2028. This page proves why, then gives you the best real options in order. Verified boundaries are labeled VERIFIED; model-derived windows resting on unannounced playoff dates are labeled ESTIMATE.

🏆 Tier 1 — Strict zero-conflict windows (the real answer)

There is exactly one kind of place a multi-day gap can hide: the late-January → mid-February playoff corridor. In January the NFL plays only on playoff weekends, so midweek days go dark; the two Sundays before the Super Bowl (Conference Championships, then the Pro Bowl Games in the bye week) chop that corridor into ~6-day stretches. After the Super Bowl, spring training starts 5 days later, leaving one more guaranteed 4-day gap. Outside that corridor, the most you ever get is 2 days (Tue–Wed pairs during football's Thu/Sun/Mon season; single dark days around the MLB All-Star break).

🧳 Tier 2 — The practical long vacation (recommended)

Two longer trips become possible if you'll forgive exactly one class of exhibition:

  • Forgive the Pro Bowl Games (the NFL's all-star exhibition): the three 2027 corridors chain into Mon Feb 1 – Sat Feb 13, 2027 — 13 days away, back home for the Super Bowl, missing only the Pro Bowl Games (~Feb 7). Same shape in 2028 (~Jan 31–Feb 12) and 2029 (~Jan 29–Feb 10). ESTIMATE (rests on unannounced playoff dates).
  • Forgive spring training (exhibition games; in PT mostly weekday afternoons ~10 AM–4:30 PM, and KNBR airs only select Giants spring games): the calendar opens up for the long trips below.
