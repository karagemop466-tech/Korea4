🗓️ How “typical day” windows are modeled

With all 30 clubs counting, what matters is the span from the day's first first-pitch to the last out (PT). The Scoreboard uses these weekday windows, labeled ESTIMATE (2027 first-pitch times are still mostly TBD):

Day typeTypical window (PT)Why
Mon, Tue, Fri3:35 PM – 10:00 PMEarliest East Coast starts ~6:35 PM ET; West Coast night games 6:40–7:15 PM PT + 2:38.
Wed, Thu9:35 AM – 10:00 PMGetaway/series-finale day games (12:35–1:05 PM ET starts) are common.
Sat10:05 AM – 10:00 PM1:05 PM ET day games through West Coast night games.
Sun10:05 AM – 7:00 PMDay games league-wide + Sunday Night Baseball 4 PM PT.
Spring Training~10:00 AM – 4:30 PMFlorida 1:05 PM ET starts (=10:05 PT); Arizona 1:05 PM MT starts. KNBR airs only select Giants spring games — but strict rules count any MLB game.
Postseason~1:05 PM – 9:00 PMWindows set by TV each round; dates are estimates until MLB publishes the calendar each August.

Individual Giants (mostly 6:45/7:15 PM PT home nights, 12:45/1:05 PM PT weekend days) and A's start times will be published by MLB over the winter; the Scoreboard's day-level free windows will tighten once they are. The vacation conclusions depend on season boundaries, which are already official for 2027.
