🔌 Practical listening notes for the Outer Sunset

  • Strong-everywhere stations: KNBR 680 (50 kW clear-channel), KNBR-FM 104.5 & KGMZ 95.7 (Mt. Sutro), KSAN 107.7 (San Bruno Mountain), KSFO 810, KNEW 960, KTCT 1050.
  • AM at night: KNBR 680 stays excellent (clear channel). KSTE 650 (A's flagship, Sacramento) is not a reliable SF signal — always use KNEW 960 for the A's.
  • KUFX 98.5 (Sharks) transmits from Mt. Umunhum south of San Jose — in the Sunset it usually works with a telescoping FM antenna aimed south/southeast; it's the first station to fade in concrete buildings.
  • Conflict rule of thumb on the Cumulus cluster: Giants/49ers keep 680/104.5; Westwood One or Stanford slides to 1050; Cal/Quakes share 810.
Station rights change with contracts (e.g., the 49ers–KNBR extension, Apr 2026). Re-verify each season. Items we could not pin to an official source are on the Flagged page, never guessed.
