Executive summary

Best vacation window: Mon Feb 15 – Thu Feb 18, 2027 — 4 full days

  • No 1-, 2- or 3-week fully-free window exists in 2027, 2028 or 2029 under your strict definition (all 30 MLB clubs + all NFL + Stanford + Cal + Westwood One). Baseball (mid-Feb–Oct), football (Aug–mid-Feb) and college football (late Aug–Jan) chain together all year.
  • The only recurring gap is between the Super Bowl and the first spring-training games each February — about 4 days in 2027 (verified anchors), and an estimated ~4–5 days in Feb 2028 and Feb 2029 (those MLB/NFL schedules are not released yet).
  • The only other full free day found is Wed Jul 14, 2027, the MLB All-Star break off-day (All-Star Game Tue Jul 13 at Wrigley Field).
  • Relaxed scenario: if spring training does not count, Feb 15 – Mar 23, 2027 (~5 weeks) is free of regular-season MLB/NFL/NCAAF games. Everything else stays the same.
  • In San Francisco (Outer Sunset, 94122) the anchor station is KNBR 680 AM / 104.5 FM — Giants, 49ers and Westwood One NFL. A's are on KNEW 960 in the Bay Area (flagship KSTE 650 Sacramento); Cal on KGO 810; Stanford historically on KNBR 1050 — see Radio page and Flagged items F7–F8.
  • Honesty note: a verified game-by-game table of every MLB/NFL game in 2027–2029 cannot exist yet — 2028/2029 schedules are unreleased and 2027 MLB start times are largely TBA. This site therefore works at verified season-anchor level and labels every estimate. Nothing is invented. See Flagged for Review (F1–F12).
