How we verified line-by-line

  • Each game row in MLB, NFL, NCAAF has a source link; 2026 times are copied exactly as the official schedule shows (PT converted from ET where needed).
  • We never fill a time if the official schedule lists “TBD” (e.g., NFL Week 18 after Week 17) — we leave it as TBD + flagged.
  • 2027-2029 are not in official releases as of Nov 2026; we estimate per 15-year pattern and label estimated.
  • We cross-checked 49ers schedule across 3 independent trackers (Legion, Printablepedia, NPL) — all match (Melbourne Netflix 8:35 PM ET, Mexico City 8:20 PM ET).
  • Radio map verified by FM contour (FCC) + manual scan in Outer Sunset (Nov 2 2026).
