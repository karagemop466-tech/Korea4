Why MLB alone kills any summer vacation week

MLB plays 162 games × 30 teams over 185 days (Mar 25 → Sep 27). That's ~2,430 total games, roughly 14–15 per day. Even on “quiet” Mondays there are 6–8 games. Every single date in-season has games — there is literally no free week until All-Star (3 days) or after the season (but then NFL overlaps). Radio window we use: 210 min (3.5h) per game — so each day has overlapping busy blocks ~10:30 AM–10:30 PM PT.

Free intraday survives: mornings 6–10:30 AM are still free on most MLB days (first pitch at 10:35 AM PT earliest). Late night after 10:30 PM is free unless West Coast extra innings — see calendar.
