All 32 teams — NFL regular season 2026

17 games per team, 18 weeks (272 regular-season games). Full schedule on NFL.com schedule (official). Every game is on some Bay Area radio combo:

  • Sun 10 AM PT: many 1 PM ET games (FOX/CBS) → not always on SF radio unless 49ers, but Westwood One doubleheader is (KNRB/680 or KGO 810).
  • Sun 1:25 PM PT: most 49ers + regional.
  • Sun 5:20 PM PT: SNF (NBC + WW1 on KGO 810) — busy even without 49ers.
  • Mon 5:15 PM PT: MNF (ESPN + WW1) — busy.
  • Thu 5:15 PM PT: TNF (Prime + WW1) — busy.
Bottom line: During Sep 10–Jan 11, Thursday + Sunday + Monday nights are always busy on KGO 810 (Westwood One) even when 49ers are on bye. That's 3 extra nights/week beyond 49ers.
