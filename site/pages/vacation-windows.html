How to use this

  • Want 1 week? Any 7 consecutive days inside Feb 9–22 (2026) works — verified.
  • Want 2 weeks? Take the full Feb 9–22 (2026). For 2027-29, book same Feb window but confirm 30 days before when MLB/NFL confirm.
  • Want 3 weeks? Not possible under strict rule. Option A: ignore MLB spring training → Feb 9–Mar 1 (2026 = 21 days). Option B: two trips: Feb + All-Star 4-day (Jul 13–16).
  • Prefer fall? No fully-free fall week exists — MLB postseason + NFL midseason make every October day busy. You'd have to skip days with games.
