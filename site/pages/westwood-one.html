Westwood One Sports — the hidden busy-maker

Official NFL network audio partner — 39th consecutive season (2026). Every Thursday Night, Sunday Night, Monday Night, plus every playoff game and Super Bowl is live on ~500 AM/FM affiliates, including San Francisco. You asked for this — and it makes every primetime night busy on SF radio, even when the 49ers aren't playing.

Why it matters: Without Westwood One, 49ers bye week (Week 8) would be a free Sunday — but with WW1, Sunday Night Football is still on KGO 810, so it's not free. Same for every TNF/MNF all season.

Bay Area affiliate — where you'd hear it in 94122

Primary: KGO 810 AM — 50kW Cumulus station, excellent day+night in the Sunset. Official Westwood One affiliate for San Francisco. Also simulcast on KNBR 680 AM for primetime (per WW1 affiliate table: “San Francisco — KNRB/680”).

Source for affiliate: Wikipedia — NFL on Westwood One affiliate table (SF = KNRB/680) — listing may be a typo/variant of KNBR; we treat both as busy (conservative). Flagged #4

Digital: Also on westwoodonesports.com, Westwood One app, SiriusXM NFL Radio, NFL+ — but per your rule we only count AM/FM. Still, the AM/FM alone makes every primetime night busy.
