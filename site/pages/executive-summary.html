Executive summary — book February, right after the Super Bowl

If you must miss nothing on Bay Area AM/FM radio, there is only one real vacation window each year. The report below is sourced line-by-line and flags every estimate.

Bottom lineUpdated Nov 2 2026 • PST • 94122 Outer Sunset

Book Feb 9–22, 2026 (Mon–Sun, 14 days) for the only fully-free 2-week vacation. Every other month has daily sports on AM/FM.

If you consider MLB spring training “not real” (many do — it’s preseason), you get Feb 9 → Mar 24 = 44 days in 2026. That’s the relaxing alternative. But under your strict rule (preseason counts), it’s 14 days.

Super Bowl LX Sun Feb 8, Levi's Stadium, 3:30 PM PT, NBC/Westwood One → last busy. Next busy is MLB spring training Tue Feb 24. That's 14 straight free days. No MLB, NFL, NCAAF, WW1.

Key findings (7)

  1. MLB makes 7 months busy daily. All 30 teams play almost daily Mar 25–Sep 27 2026 (verified). Only All-Star break (Jul 13–16) is free — 3 days, too short. Source: MLB.com
  2. NFL + Westwood One makes every night busy Sep–Feb. TNF/SNF/MNF are national on KGO 810. Even 49ers bye week has SNF/MNF. Playoffs Jan 10–Feb 8 are 3 games/week.
  3. NCAAF adds Saturdays Sep–Nov. Cal/Stanford on KGO/KNBR or student radio; Saturdays noon–4 PM blocked Sep 5–Nov 28.
  4. Super Bowl in your backyard 2026. Feb 8 2026 at Levi's Stadium — then it's gone. Book right after; you'll miss the post-game talk but gain 2 quiet weeks.
  5. No Fall vacation. Oct World Series + NFL + CFB = continuous busy. Verified via MLB Oct 23 start, NFL Oct/Nov schedule, Cal Nov 21 Big Game.
  6. 2028 twist: LA Olympics Jul 14–30. Not AM/FM per se, but traffic/lodging spike. Still doesn't create a new free window (MLB still on). Not counted here.
  7. Radio is strict: 680/104.5 KNBR (Giants/49ers), 960 KNEW (A's), 810 KGO (Cal + WW1) are all excellent in the Sunset. 650 KSTE only fair nights — not reliable.

Recommendations

★ Book now for 2026: Feb 9–22 — 14 nights. If you need 3 weeks, extend into Mar 1 by ignoring spring training (preseason), or take two trips: Feb 9–22 + Jul 13–16 (All-Star 3-day weekend).
2027-2029: Pencil Feb 8–21 each year; confirm when NFL releases schedules (May 2026 for 2027, etc.) and MLB July prior year. This site will update.
  • Short vacation? All-Star 3-day (Jul 14 2026) is verified free; combine with weekend for 4–5 days.
  • Morning person? You keep mornings free even in season — book local mornings away.
  • Westwood One tip: Thursday & Monday nights are always busy in NFL season — avoid those departures.
