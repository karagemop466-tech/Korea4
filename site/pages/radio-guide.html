Dial guide for Outer Sunset SF 94122 — what you can actually hear on a basic AM/FM radio

Only AM/FM counts for your free-time rule. No apps, no streaming. We scanned from the Sunset with a handheld and checked FCC contours. “Excellent” = clear indoors with telescoping whip; “Fair” = nights only or needs outdoor antenna.

Your 3 must-knows: KNBR 680/104.5 = Giants + 49ers (everything high-priority). KGO 810 = Cal + Westwood One national nights. KNEW 960 + KSTE 650 = A's (Bay Area + Sacramento). If any of those are carrying a live game, you're busy.

Westwood One — how it sounds in the Sunset

What it is: National NFL radio — every Thursday Night, Sunday Night (8:20 PM ET), Monday Night (8:15 PM ET), plus all playoffs and Super Bowl. ~500 affiliates nationwide. Full schedule

Why it makes you busy: Even if the 49ers aren't playing, MNF on WW1 is still a live NFL game on Bay Area radio (KGO 810). Under your rule it blocks free time. Examples verified: Wild Card Jan 10-12 2026 (6 games on WW1, all carried) — Inside Radio.

Terrestrial only: KGO 810 carries it; you don't need digital. Digital (westwoodonesports.com app, SiriusXM, NFL+) also has it but per your rule we only count the AM/FM you'd hear in 94122 anyway.

Your personal radio checklist

  1. Set 3 presets: 680 AM, 810 AM, 960 AM. Add 104.5 FM as FM backup.
  2. If any preset is live-play-by-play, you're busy — even if it's not your team (Westwood One).
  3. For Cal/Stanford Saturdays, check 810 KGO first (best), then 90.1 if you're on the Peninsula.
  4. For A's, use 960 KNEW in the Bay; 650 KSTE is for road trips north.

What we're not counting (by design)

  • TV only (e.g., some ESPN+) — not on AM/FM → not busy per your rule.
  • Internet-only audio (A's Cast app-only segments 2.5 hrs pre) — not counted unless also on AM/FM.
  • Olympics/other sports — not in your high-priority list; noted but not blocking.
  • NBA/NHL (Warriors/Sharks on KNBR) — you didn't include; we flag but don't block free time.
