Ranking metrics

Metric definitions used to rank strategies and to separate skill from luck.

MetricDefinitionStatusOfficial source
Brier scoreMean squared error of predicted probabilities; lower is better. Skill baseline = 0.25 for a 50/50 coin flip.FRAMEWORKNFL.com official stats
Log lossCross-entropy of predicted probabilities; lower is better; penalises confident wrong calls.FRAMEWORKNFL.com official stats
ROIReturn on turnover: (profit or loss) / total stake across settled bets.FRAMEWORKNFL.com official stats
Hit rateShare of settled bets that win, by bet type.FRAMEWORKNFL.com official stats
Skill vs. luckSignificance test of performance vs. a no-skill baseline; separates selective skill from variance.FRAMEWORKNFL.com official playing rules
Sample sizeCount of settled, out-of-sample bets; gates ranking until the minimum is met.FRAMEWORKNFL.com official stats
