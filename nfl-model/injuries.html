How to read the official report

Three rules that determine almost every bug in an injury pipeline.

  • Practice status and game status are different fields with different meanings; the official definitions are Did Not Participate / Limited Participation (under 100% of normal reps) / Full Participation (100%) for practice, and Out / Doubtful / Questionable for game status.
  • A blank Game Status is NOT missing data: the league policy states that a player certain to play is deliberately not listed in the Game Status Report, and that deactivating such a player requires an explanation to the league.
  • The official page shows a single practice status per player (the latest report), not the Wednesday/Thursday/Friday ladder, and its Injuries column is frequently blank; we record what the page shows and leave the rest blank.
