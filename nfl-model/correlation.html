Correlation values and how they get earned

Parlays and prop combos are joint probabilities. This page records what dependence we can state today, and the measurement path for the rest.

No correlation value is published in the 'measured' column, because none is measured. The column is blank by the same rule that blanks a market price; the only values shown are the ones arithmetic can produce from the marginals, and they are labelled as such.

Estimation method and thresholds

How a measured value gets here, and the minimum evidence before it may enter a price.

ItemStatementState
EstimatorPearson correlation of binary outcomes across our own resolved tickets, reported with a season-block bootstrap interval; for continuous prop pairs, correlation of realised values against projections (residual correlation), which is what a joint pricing model actually needs.SPECIFIED
Bounds fallbackWhere no estimate exists, the only defensible statement is the Fréchet-Hoeffding bound on the joint probability implied by the two marginals; the builder computes the corresponding correlation bounds so the reader can see how useless they are for pricing (see the bounds table on this page).SPECIFIED
Why not import published valuesA published correlation from another operator, another season or another scoring rule is not our measurement: it does not come with our marginals, our cut-off or our settlement definitions, and the site's own verification rule forbids laundering it into a verified row.VERIFIED - POLICY
Minimum evidence per pairAt least 300 resolved tickets per pair before a measured value may be used in a price, and only if the bootstrap interval excludes the operator's implied value at the registered level.SPECIFIED
