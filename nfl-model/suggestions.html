SG-08 · Model — AWAITING-DECISION

Key numbers (3, 7, 10 in NFL) make spread and total edges asymmetric; a model blind to them is miscalibrated by construction.

Proposal: Model the margin distribution explicitly and evaluate half-point and key-number shifts as a priced asset (buying a half point has a known cost in vig and a known value in win probability), rather than treating the spread as continuous.

A - Discrete margin distribution + key-number adjustment table
Effort: Medium · Cost: $0
Risk: Needs many seasons of margins; the public academic sample we cite is 2002-2011 and is not current.
B - Normal-approx margin model
Effort: Low · Cost: $0
Risk: Systematically underprices the mass at 3 and 7.
C - Copy a public power rating
Effort: none needed · Cost: $0
Risk: We then have someone else's edge, priced against their own line; no independent origination at all.

Recommendation: option A — The whole point of originating is the discrete distribution; B and C defeat it.

Decision required from you: Approve building the margin-distribution estimator from official box scores (no data purchase needed for this one).

If approved, it touches: scripts/build_nfl_model.py, new scripts/model_margin.py

SG-14 · Competition — AWAITING-DECISION
