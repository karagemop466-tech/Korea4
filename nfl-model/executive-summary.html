Executive summary

The one-page read for whoever decides: what is verified, what is missing, what it costs, and what to do first.

Bottom line. The NFL line-origination programme is now specified, documented and verifiable - and it has no results, because results require one purchased input that no amount of modelling can substitute for. That is the single most important fact for planning: the work to date removes ambiguity about what to build and in what order, not about whether the market can be beaten.

Recommended path, in order

Sequenced so that each step is falsifiable before the next one spends money.

  1. Answer Q-02 (objective) and Q-06 (jurisdiction/eligibility) - free, and they decide everything downstream.
  2. Approve SG-01 option A: a short, logged price-feed trial through the remaining 2026 weeks. No archive purchase until the trial proves coverage.
  3. Build the free half now: box-score ingestion (LM-06), 32-club chart and report collection (LM-03/LM-04), and the discrete margin model (SG-08). None of it needs a purchase.
  4. Start Tier 1 paper logging with the frozen gate set and pre-registered specs (SG-06, G-01..G-04). No strategy is funded on the first season, ever.
  5. Only after a season of CLV and calibration evidence: pick the funding policy (Q-16), then choose a reward model (SG-14), then legal review (Q-13).
  6. Keep the site's honesty machinery mandatory: per-dataset staleness (SG-18/SG-05), gate-enforced disclosures (SG-17), and blanks rather than guesses in every result column.
