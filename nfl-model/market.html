Why a state's tax figure is not a hold percentage. The Colorado release publishes handle and tax on the same page, but tax is a share of adjusted gross revenue, and the report that contains revenue by bet type is a separate operator-level PDF (Q-04). Nothing on this site converts a tax number into a hold claim.
