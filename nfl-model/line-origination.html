Origination pipeline and market arithmetic

How an independent line gets produced, what it needs, and the arithmetic that decides whether a claim about it can ever be tested.

The honest summary of this page: the pipeline is specified end to end and most of its inputs are official and free; the one input that cannot be synthesised is the market price, and until it exists no number on this site can be called an edge.

Derived arithmetic — how big must the edge be to be provable

Bets needed to detect a true edge over break-even, normal approximation, two-sided 5% significance and 80% power, variance evaluated at the -110 break-even probability.

True edge over break-evenBets neededSeasons at 272 bets/seasonPractical read
0.5 pts78,311287.9not separable from noise inside any single-season plan
1.0 pts19,57872.0needs roughly 18 seasons of once-per-game betting
2.0 pts4,89418.0needs roughly 4-5 seasons - the realistic floor for a funded side
3.0 pts2,1758.0about 2 seasons at one bet per game, or one season at 4-5 independent bets per game
5.0 pts7832.9one season is enough - which is why such edges are usually already priced out
8.0 pts3061.1a season of a handful of bets; a number this large in a public market is a red flag about the estimate, not the opportunity

n = (z(0.975) + z(0.80))^2 * p0(1-p0) / edge^2 with p0 = 110/210 = 0.52381. Independent bets assumed: correlated bets, parlays and a 272-game season all make these numbers worse, never better.
