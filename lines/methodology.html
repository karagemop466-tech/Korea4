Methodology

How the numbers were produced. Written before the strategy results were read.

Own line model (walk-forward)

  • Margin: ridge regression of home minus away points on team strengths plus a home-field term (penalty 20, not tuned). Total: ridge on offence and defence terms (penalty 20).
  • Each week's games are predicted using only games played before that week. No same-week or future information.
  • Recency weighting: 365-day half-life.

Grading

  • Regular-season games with final scores and a closing spread or total.
  • Spread: home covers when margin is greater than the spread_line; push on equality. Total: over when the total is greater than the line.
  • Stake 1 unit at an assumed −110 (win +0.9091, loss −1). Breakeven 52.38%.

Validation gates

  1. Sample size ≥ 400 graded bets.
  2. Hit rate ≥ breakeven and Wilson 95% lower bound > 50%.
  3. One-sided exact binomial p vs breakeven, Holm-adjusted across all strategies, < 0.05.
  4. Positive ROI in ≥ 60% of seasons.
  5. Hit rate above the 95th percentile of 1,000 random-side simulations.
  6. 2026 forward-slice ROI > 0 (reported, not used to choose strategies).

Correlation and parlays

  • phi (Pearson on 0/1 outcomes) with Fisher-z 95% confidence intervals.
  • Parlay joint probabilities are observed on games where both legs were placed. No independence assumption is used for the observed column.

Limits

  • No official betting-line source, no injury history, no depth charts, no player props. These are the largest gaps (see Questions).
  • Results describe past data only and are not predictions.
