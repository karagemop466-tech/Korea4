Sources & radio

Where the schedule comes from and how to read it.

◷  Pacific time · PST / PDT

Executive summary

The daily view combines a live query to MLB’s official schedule feed with a small, manually checked set of official 49ers, college, NBA and Westwood One listings. It is not a comprehensive feed for all leagues or all Bay Area stations. All MLB and NFL games are within the requested conflict scope even when local terrestrial carriage is unconfirmed. The displayed gaps are not verified free time.

How the estimates work

For timed MLB games the planner adds 158 minutes to first pitch: MLB reported a 2h 38m average for nine-inning games through September 25, 2025. For 49ers games it uses 210 minutes after kickoff; for Westwood One broadcasts 240 minutes after their listed on-air start; for NBA 150 minutes after tipoff. These are explicitly arbitrary planning assumptions, not verified averages or end times. Actual games, overtime, delays, and radio pre/postgame can extend beyond them. Halftime is inside the occupied interval, not free time. A TBD start makes the whole day uncertain. All calculations use America/Los_Angeles, switching between PST and PDT automatically.
