Leaderboard competition framework. A rewarded, gated strategy competition for the site's own NFL numbers — singles, parlays, and player-prop combos. Scoring, gates and rewards below are labelled proposals; none has been run. The league's own official precedent for a rewarded analytics competition is the Big Data Bowl ($100,000 shared prize, Next Gen Stats data).

Competition design

Deterministic, documented rule sets; division-scoped leaderboards; rewards gated on validation. Reward values are intentionally absent until the owner decides (Q-04).

Validation gates

A strategy earns leaderboard eligibility only after every gate passes. Luck is removed mechanically: closing-line benchmarking (V-03) plus the shuffled-selection significance test (V-07) score selective skill, not fortunate sequences. Until an odds source is selected, gates that need market data stay blocked (IR-025).
