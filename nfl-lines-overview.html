First decisions to unblock the programme

  1. Price source: connect a timestamped bet log or licensed feed; absent that, keep the competition unscored.
  2. Availability: obtain dated official depth captures for all clubs before applying comparative penalties.
  3. Historical panel: capture past official injury reports and time-stamped sources before validating injury strategies.
  4. Exposure policy: set bankroll and weekly risk limits before any competition is actionable.
