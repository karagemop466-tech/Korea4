What a "signal" is here. A measurable factor evaluated against completed game outcomes from committed data. Everything is computed from the 2026 season to date (n = 137 games) — single-season, small-sample, labelled ESTIMATED. The rating model is margin = k × (home net-EPA/play − away net-EPA/play) + h₀, fitted by ordinary least squares on completed games; EPA is the officially defined Next Gen Stats measure.
