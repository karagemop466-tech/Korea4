Executive summary — what to actually book

★ When to go

The only week+ windows with zero radio sports are the 2 weeks right after the Super Bowl. In 2026 that's Feb 9–22 (Mon–Sun). Same pattern Feb 8–21-ish in 2027-29 (estimated). There is no 3-week fully-free window under your rules. All-Star break is only 3 days.

If you're willing to ignore MLB spring training (it's preseason), you get 5–6 weeks: Feb 9 → Mar 24 (verified 2026). Most people do this — spring training is low-priority.

Why it's this tight

MLB = 162 games/30 teams, Mar 25–Sep 27 → busy almost daily for 7 months. NFL adds Sep–Feb (Westwood One makes every primetime night busy). NCAAF adds Sep–Nov. Together they leave only one gap: post-Super Bowl → spring training. Even holidays like July 4 have all 30 MLB teams playing (2026: every team plays July 4 for America's 250th).

Game durations used: MLB 2:48–3:10 +30 pre (→210 min window), NFL 3:12 +60 pre (→282 min), NCAAF 3:27 (→252 min). Method
