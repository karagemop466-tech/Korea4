How to use these lines (process, not advice)

  1. Compute the model line before looking at any market number; log both with timestamps (G5 no-lookahead).
  2. Only differences ≥ 1.0 point (spread) or ≥ 1.5 points (total) are recorded as review candidates — threshold is a documented research convention flagged under Q10.
  3. When a market-reference feed is approved (Q1), every decision logs price-taken vs closing line for G7; until then no strategy can be certified.
  4. Multi-leg tickets must pass through the correlation adjustment on nfl-correlation.html (G6) with a 2-leg same-game cap.
  5. Grading is always from committed boxscores after the game — never re-derived lines.

Key numbers (documented convention, unfitted)

NFL scoring concentrates on 3- and 7-point increments; spread decisions near 3 and 7 are treated as key-number territory when logging edges. The distribution is refitable once multi-season history is committed (Q8) — no fitted key-number table is published yet because one season (n=137) is too small; publishing one would violate the no-inference rule.
