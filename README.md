# FREE TIME VACATION 📻

**When is *nothing* you care about on Bay Area sports radio?**

A day-by-day free-time planner for a San Francisco (Outer Sunset, 94122) AM/FM radio listener,
covering **Nov 2, 2026 – Dec 31, 2029**. A minute is **free** when none of these are on the air:

- **MLB** — all 30 clubs, incl. spring training & postseason (★ Giants on KNBR 680/104.5, ★ A's on KNEW 960)
- **NFL** — all games incl. preseason/playoffs (★ 49ers on KNBR 680/104.5 & 107.7 The Bone)
- **Stanford ★** (KTCT 1050) and **California ★** (810 AM) NCAA football
- **Westwood One** national NFL radio (Thursday Night Football/SNF/MNF → Super Bowl, on KNBR/KTCT)

## Headline findings
- **No strict 1/2/3-week zero-conflict vacation window exists in 2027–2029.** The dial's longest fully-dark
  stretches are **6 consecutive days**, always in the late-Jan → mid-Feb NFL-playoff corridor — e.g.
  **Feb 1–6 and Feb 8–13, 2027** (estimates), beside the one both-sides-verified window **Feb 15–18, 2027**
  (Super Bowl LXI → first spring-training games, per official NFL & MLB dates).
- Conditional best of the decade: **Jul 12–20, 2028 (9 days)** *if* MLB finalizes its envisioned extended
  Olympic break during LA28 — not agreed yet.
- Practical long trips: the "Super Bowl sandwich" (miss only the Pro Bowl Games) or Super-Bowl-to-Opening-
  Day (only spring training on air). Full rankings: `docs/vacation.html`.
