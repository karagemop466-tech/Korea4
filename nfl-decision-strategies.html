Why the leaderboard is a leaderboard and not a tip sheet

  • Ranking uses closing-line value, so a strategy is rewarded for beating a price, not for a hot streak.
  • The score subtracts each strategy's own 5th-percentile bootstrap result, so variance is penalised explicitly.
  • Parlays and prop combos are separate contestants with their measured correlation attached — a combo cannot hide a correlated leg.
  • Reward currency is left blank (LB-08) because it is your decision, and the legal question behind it is flagged (Q-19).
