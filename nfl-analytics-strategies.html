Leaderboard purpose: remove luck, reward selective skill. A strategy earns nothing from a lucky streak: gates require provenance, sample size, luck-adjusted significance (t-stat), held-out persistence, no lookahead, correlation accounting, and (once prices exist) closing-line discipline. Rewards are points and tiers — never money. SIMULATION ONLY. No real market prices are ingested; the -110 assumption is a research convention for comparing strategy hit rates against breakeven (52.38% at -110).

Player-prop combos (policy)

Player-prop combos are supported by the same gates. Prop baselines (usage, snap share, per-game distributions) come from committed snap-count and play-by-play snapshots; prop LINES are market data and are NOT ingested under the current official-only rule (open question Q1/Q7). Until prop lines are available, prop strategies can be registered and graded only on model-side probability estimates, which cannot pass G7 — they remain Spec tier.

Registration

Required fields: id, name, rule (timestamped, mechanical), market_dependency (none | market-reference), legs (single | parlay; if parlay: leg list + correlation method), stake_policy (flat 1 unit for research grading), owner, registered_utc. Registrations are JSON objects appended to data/nfl-analytics/strategies.json via a reviewed commit; the registry is append-only — rules are never edited after registration, replaced strategies get a new id.

Every table row links to an official source. Blank official values are flagged UNVERIFIED. Estimates are never presented as released schedules.
