What it would take to turn a FAIL into a PASS

  • G4 sample sufficiency — capture final scores from NFL.com weekly pages; 200 games is roughly eight weeks of slates.
  • G5 holdout — freeze the rule on 2026 and test on 2027, which means capturing inputs for a whole season first.
  • G6 market benchmark — a licensed closing-line history, or an explicit decision to drop profitability claims (D-03/D-04).
  • G7 luck separation — depends entirely on G4; the bootstrap code is straightforward once settled bets exist.
  • G8 correlation control — requires measured correlations on settled legs; the pre-registered threshold is D-08.
