⚠️ Important Notes on Vacation Planning

  • The "sweet spot" is between the end of the World Series (late October) and the start of NFL mid-season, or between the Super Bowl (mid-February) and MLB Opening Day (late March).
  • During MLB season (late March - late September): Very difficult to find free days as games are played nearly every day.
  • During NFL season (September - January): Sundays, Mondays, and Thursdays are consistently busy. Some mid-week stretches may be free.
  • During NCAAF season (late August - early December): Saturdays are consistently busy.
  • 2027-2029 schedules are estimated based on historical patterns. Official schedules are typically released 6-12 months in advance.
  • Westwood One broadcasts cover all primetime NFL games nationally, receivable on KNBR 680 AM in SF.
