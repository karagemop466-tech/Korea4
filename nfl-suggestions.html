Decision-readiness checklist

  1. Before any odds-dependent work (CLV, correlations, parlay pricing): Q-02 odds source + Q-03 jurisdiction review + Q-06 budget. Until then, correlation cells stay blank (IR-025) and market gates stay blocked.
  2. Before any reward is paid or claimed: Q-04 reward design + all gates V-01…V-08 run and passed for the strategy family.
  3. Before any player-prop division goes live: Q-05 market-scope review against the official prohibited-wager categories.
  4. Before depth metrics become ratings: Q-08 depth-order authority + Q-09 participation source.
  5. Before any site change from a suggestion: a decision record on COORDINATION.md referencing the suggestion ID.

Suggestions for discovering the missing information

  1. Capture the official injury report every reporting day (S-03) — it also builds the longitudinal data the backtests need.
  2. Diff official schedule pages against captured rows weekly (S-01) — catches IR-022-class changes within days.
  3. Survey official club sites for depth charts/weekly depth announcements, one club at a time, recording what exists (Q-08).
  4. Investigate official NGS participation products for a per-player weekly usage feed (Q-09).
  5. Keep every new fact tied to a dated, linked official source; leave everything else blank — the repo's existing rule.
