📐 Methodology

  • All 2026 data is sourced from official team/league websites and verified radio station schedules.
  • 2027-2029 dates are estimated based on historical patterns (MLB starts late March, NFL starts early September, etc.).
  • Game durations are based on 2025 league averages from official reports.
  • All times are converted to PST (UTC-8) for the Outer Sunset SF 94122 area.
  • "Free" means no tracked sport is broadcasting live on any AM/FM station receivable in the area.
  • Vacation windows are consecutive days with zero tracked sports broadcasts.
  • Estimated items are clearly marked with ~ Estimated badges.
  • Verified items are marked with ✓ Verified badges and include source links.
